About Elgro

Elgro is an agricultural technology platform dedicated to helping farmers protect their crops and maximize yields through fast, accurate disease diagnosis and expert recommendations.

Founded with a mission to support food security and reduce hunger, we combine local agricultural expertise with modern technology to provide farmers with accessible, reliable crop health solutions.

Our platform connects farmers with verified agricultural knowledge, local suppliers, and a supportive farming community - all designed to help you grow healthier crops and increase your income.

Our Commitment: Expert-verified diagnoses within 24 hours, local-specific recommendations, and continuous support throughout your farming season.
